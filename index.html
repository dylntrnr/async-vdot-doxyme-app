Access the clinician dashboard to review your patient queue, check adherence rates, manage enrollments, and approve or flag recorded doses — all without scheduling a live call.
How It Works
1
Enroll Patient
Add patient to roster with medication schedule, frequency, and recording instructions.
2
Patient Records Dose
Patient opens their link, follows the guided flow, and records a short video of themselves taking their medication.
3
Clinician Reviews Queue
Videos appear in the review queue. Watch, approve, flag for follow-up, or request a retake — in minutes.
4
Adherence Tracked Automatically
Dashboard shows 7-day trends, streak data, and completion rates across your entire patient panel.
